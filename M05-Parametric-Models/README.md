## M05A — Probabilistic models: HMM and Naive Bayes

- [Open the notebook](M05A-Probabilistic-Models-HMM-and-Naive-Bayes.ipynb)
- [Open in Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/tulip-lab/pattern-classification-lab/blob/develop/M05-Parametric-Models/M05A-Probabilistic-Models-HMM-and-Naive-Bayes.ipynb)
- Alignment: CLO1, CLO2, CLO3
- Duration: approximately 2 hours
- Review status: executable candidate; licensing recorded; publication pending

Learners compare the conditional-independence assumptions of Gaussian Naive
Bayes and a first-order HMM, evaluate a supervised classifier, and decode a
state sequence using a log-space Viterbi implementation.

Prerequisites are conditional probability, logarithms, products of
probabilities, and basic supervised classification. Iris is bundled with
scikit-learn; the HMM sequence is defined in the notebook.
