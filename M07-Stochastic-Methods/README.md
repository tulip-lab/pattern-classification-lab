## M07A — From simulation to decision

- [Open the notebook](M07A-Stochastic-Methods-from-Simulation-to-Decision.ipynb)

- Duration: approximately 3 hours, or two shorter practicals at the MCMC–GP bridge
- Review status: executable candidate; licensing recorded; publication pending

One synthetic smart-greenhouse case carries learners from Monte Carlo risk
estimation through Metropolis–Hastings, Gibbs sampling, chain diagnostics,
Gaussian-process regression, GP temporal forecasting, and Bayesian
optimisation. Each method adds evidence to the same decision ledger.

Prerequisites are random variables, Normal distributions, conditional
probability, means and variances, basic Python/NumPy, and reading a line chart.
The guided path uses deterministic synthetic data and requires no download.
