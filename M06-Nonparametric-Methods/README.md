## M06A — Nonparametric classification: Parzen and KNN

- Duration: approximately 2 hours
- Review status: executable candidate; licensing recorded; publication pending

Learners build class-conditional kernel-density and nearest-neighbour
classifiers, compare them under a shared protocol, and relate bandwidth and
neighbour count to bias, variance, and boundary smoothness.

Prerequisites are distances, neighbourhoods, conditional probability, scaling,
and train/test splitting. The guided path uses synthetic data and requires no
download.
